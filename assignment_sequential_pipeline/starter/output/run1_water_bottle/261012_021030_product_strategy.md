# Product Strategy
## Product Name
HydrateTrack

## Value Proposition
HydrateTrack is an affordable, customizable, and smart hydration solution that helps university students and health enthusiasts stay properly hydrated throughout the day without constantly checking their phones.

## MVP Features
### Must-have
1. **Hydration Tracking:** Automatically tracks water intake and provides real-time reminders.
2. **Customizable Designs:** Offers a variety of designs to appeal to different tastes.
3. **Integration with Health Apps:** Seamlessly integrates with popular health and fitness apps.
4. **Affordable Pricing:** Provides a budget-friendly option compared to other smart water bottles.

### Nice-to-have
1. **Durable Build:** Made from high-quality, durable materials to withstand daily use.
2. **Bluetooth Connectivity:** Ensures reliable connectivity for tracking and reminders.
3. **Long Battery Life:** Extended battery life to minimize the need for frequent charging.

## Pricing Tiers
| Tier       | Price (USD) | Who it is for |
|------------|--------------|---------------|
| Basic      | 34.50        | Budget-conscious students |
| Plus       | 46.00        | Students seeking more features |
| Premium    | 57.50        | Health enthusiasts |

Assumptions: cost USD 25, margin 45%.

## Positioning Statement
HydrateTrack is the go-to smart hydration solution for university students and health enthusiasts who want to stay properly hydrated without breaking the bank or constantly checking their phones.
