# Customer Personas

## Persona 1: Emily, University Student
- Age: 21
- Background: Emily is a 21-year-old sophomore majoring in Biology at a large university. She is active in campus clubs and often finds herself forgetting to drink enough water.
- Goals: Emily wants to stay hydrated to maintain her energy levels and focus during long study sessions and club meetings.
- Pain Points: Emily struggles with remembering to drink water throughout the day, which sometimes leads to dehydration and fatigue.
- Buying Triggers: Emily is motivated to buy a smart water bottle that is affordable, has customizable designs, and integrates with her fitness app.
- Quote: "I wish there was an easy way to track my water intake without constantly checking my phone."


## Persona 2: Jake, College Athlete
- Age: 20
- Background: Jake is a 20-year-old basketball player on his university's varsity team. He needs to stay hydrated to perform at his best during practices and games.
- Goals: Jake aims to optimize his hydration to improve his athletic performance and recovery.
- Pain Points: Jake often forgets to drink enough water, especially during intense training sessions, which affects his performance.
- Buying Triggers: Jake is looking for a smart water bottle that is durable, affordable, and can track his hydration in real-time.
- Quote: "I need a bottle that can keep up with my busy schedule and help me stay on top of my hydration."


## Persona 3: Sarah, Health Enthusiast
- Age: 23
- Background: Sarah is a 23-year-old graduate student who is passionate about health and wellness. She enjoys working out and wants to ensure she is drinking enough water to support her active lifestyle.
- Goals: Sarah wants to maintain her health and wellness by staying properly hydrated.
- Pain Points: Sarah sometimes forgets to drink water, which can lead to dehydration and hinder her fitness goals.
- Buying Triggers: Sarah is attracted to a smart water bottle that is affordable, has a sleek design, and integrates with her health apps.
- Quote: "I love tracking my fitness, but it would be great if my water intake was included in that."
