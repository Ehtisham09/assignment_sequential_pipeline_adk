Frequently Asked Questions

How does Stride build my study schedule?

Simply upload your course syllabus. Stride automatically extracts assignments, exam weightings, and due dates, transforming them into a realistic day-by-day study roadmap.

How much does Stride cost?

Stride offers three plans: Free ($0/month) with standard scheduling and one syllabus upload; Pro ($8/month) with unlimited syllabi, dynamic rebalancing, and fatigue-aware pacing; and Team ($18/user/month) for study groups and cohorts.

What happens if I miss a study session?

You never need to rebuild your calendar manually. Tap rebalance, and Stride instantly recalculates your upcoming free blocks to keep you on track.
