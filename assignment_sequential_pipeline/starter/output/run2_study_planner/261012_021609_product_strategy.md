# Product Strategy
## Product Name
ExamPlanner

## Value Proposition
ExamPlanner simplifies exam preparation by generating personalized daily revision schedules based on your exam timetable, helping you manage your study time effectively and reduce exam stress.

## MVP Features
### Must-have
1. **AI-driven Personalized Schedules**: Generates daily revision plans based on the user's exam timetable.
2. **Integration with Exam Timetables**: Directly imports exam timetables from various sources.
3. **User-friendly Interface**: Simplifies the study planning process with an intuitive design.
4. **Progress Tracking**: Allows users to track their study progress and adjust plans accordingly.

### Nice-to-have
1. **Notifications and Reminders**: Sends reminders for upcoming study sessions and exams.
2. **Social Sharing**: Enables users to share their study plans with friends and peers.
3. **Analytics Dashboard**: Provides insights into study habits and performance.

## Pricing Tiers
| Tier | Price (USD) | Who it is for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic | 42.0 | High school students and casual users |
| Plus | 56.0 | University students and regular users |
| Premium | 70.0 | Professional exam takers and power users |

Assumptions: cost USD 50, margin 40%.

## Positioning Statement
ExamPlanner is the go-to study planner for students of all ages, offering a personalized, user-friendly solution to manage exam schedules and reduce study stress.
