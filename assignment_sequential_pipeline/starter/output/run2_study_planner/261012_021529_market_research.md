# Market Research Report
## Product Idea
The product idea is an AI study planner app that generates a daily revision schedule based on a student's exam timetable.

## Problem Statement
The core problem this product solves is the difficulty students face in managing their study time effectively around their exam schedule. It benefits students most, especially those who struggle with time management and creating a structured study plan.

## Target Market
### Primary Segment
- High school and university students preparing for exams.
### Secondary Segment
- Adult learners returning to education or taking professional exams.
### Market Size
The market is large and growing, as digital learning and the need for efficient study tools increase with the global shift towards remote education and lifelong learning.

## Competitor Analysis
| Competitor | Strengths | Weaknesses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Competitor A (e.g., MyStudyLife) | Strong user base, detailed calendar integration | High complexity, not specifically tailored for exams |
| Competitor B (e.g., Google Calendar) | Ubiquitous access, simple interface | Lacks AI-driven personalization, no specific study focus |
| Competitor C (e.g., Trello) | Flexible, visual task management | No time management features, not exam-focused |

## Market Gaps & Opportunities
1. Lack of AI-driven personalization specifically for exam schedules.
2. Absence of features that integrate directly with exam timetables to create revision plans.
3. Need for a user-friendly interface that simplifies the study planning process for students.

## Risks & Mitigations
| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| Market Risk (low demand for specialized exam planning) | Conduct surveys and focus groups to validate the product idea and refine the target market |
| Technical Risk (difficulty in integrating with various exam timetable formats) | Develop a flexible API that can adapt to different timetable formats and regularly update it |
| Regulatory Risk (data privacy concerns) | Implement strong data protection measures and comply with relevant regulations like GDPR |
