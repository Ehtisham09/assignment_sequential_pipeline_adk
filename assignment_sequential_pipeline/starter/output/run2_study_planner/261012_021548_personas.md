# Customer Personas

## Persona 1: Alice Johnson
- Age: 18
- Background: High school senior preparing for SATs and AP exams.
- Goals: To balance schoolwork and extracurricular activities while maintaining good grades. To create a study schedule that helps her manage her time effectively. To reduce stress and anxiety related to exams.
- Pain Points: Difficulty in managing time between school, sports, and social life. Struggles to create a balanced study schedule. Feels overwhelmed by the number of exams.
- Buying Triggers: Recommendations from friends who found the app helpful. Positive reviews highlighting the app's ability to create personalized study plans. The app's integration with her school's exam timetable.
- Quote: "I need a way to organize my study time so I can focus on what really matters - passing my exams and enjoying my high school experience."


## Persona 2: Mark Thompson
- Age: 35
- Background: Adult learner returning to university for a Master's degree in Data Science.
- Goals: To efficiently manage study time alongside a full-time job. To create a study schedule that allows for consistent progress. To reduce the stress of balancing work and studies.
- Pain Points: Difficulty in finding time to study between work and personal life. Struggles to create a study schedule that fits into his busy lifestyle. Feels stressed by the need to balance professional and academic commitments.
- Buying Triggers: The app's ability to integrate with his university's exam timetable. Positive reviews from other adult learners. The app's flexibility in creating schedules for non-traditional students.
- Quote: "I need a study planner that understands the challenges of balancing work and study - this app seems like the solution."


## Persona 3: Sarah Lee
- Age: 22
- Background: University student preparing for final exams in her Computer Science degree.
- Goals: To create a detailed study schedule that covers all subjects. To ensure she revises effectively without feeling overwhelmed. To improve her exam performance.
- Pain Points: Difficulty in prioritizing subjects and topics for revision. Struggles to find a balance between studying for different exams. Feels overwhelmed by the volume of material to cover.
- Buying Triggers: The app's AI-driven personalization and ability to create detailed revision plans. Positive word-of-mouth from peers who have used the app. The app's user-friendly interface.
- Quote: "I need a study planner that can help me prioritize my revision and make sure I cover everything without feeling stressed."
