# Product Strategy
## Product Name
**HydraUni**

## Value Proposition
Stay hydrated effortlessly with HydraUni, a smart water bottle that tracks your hydration levels, integrates with campus services, and offers personalized goals to help university students maintain optimal hydration for better health and performance.

## MVP Features
### Must-have
1. **Hydration Tracking:** Monitor water intake throughout the day.
2. **Personalized Goals:** Set and adjust hydration goals based on individual needs and activities.
3. **Integration with Campus Services:** Connect with meal plans and health services for a comprehensive wellness approach.

### Nice-to-have
1. **Social Features:** Challenge friends and track hydration in groups.
2. **Bluetooth Connectivity:** Sync data with fitness apps for a holistic health view.
3. **Long Battery Life:** Ensure continuous tracking without frequent recharging.

## Pricing Tiers
| Tier | Price (USD) | Who it is for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic | 45.38 | Students looking for essential hydration tracking. |
| Plus | 60.50 | Students wanting additional features like social challenges. |
| Premium | 75.63 | Students seeking full integration with campus services and advanced tracking. |

Assumptions: cost USD 30, margin 45%.

## Positioning Statement
HydraUni is the go-to smart water bottle for university students who want to stay hydrated, healthy, and connected with their peers while achieving their academic and athletic goals.
