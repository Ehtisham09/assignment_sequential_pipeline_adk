# Product Strategy

## Product Name
Stride

## Value Proposition
For university students managing demanding academic workloads, Stride provides an AI-driven adaptive scheduling system that automatically converts course syllabi into realistic, flexible daily study plans.

## MVP Features

### Must-Have
1. **Automated Syllabus & Deadline Ingestion:** Extracts course timelines, assignment deadlines, and exam weightings directly from uploaded syllabi to generate initial study schedules.
   - *Persona:* Maya Lin
   - *Pain Point Solved:* Spending excessive time manually creating study planners only to abandon them when course demands shift.
2. **Dynamic One-Tap Schedule Rebalancing:** Instantly reschedules missed or delayed study blocks across upcoming free windows without manual calendar rearranging.
   - *Persona:* Marcus Vance
   - *Pain Point Solved:* Late-running professional obligations and workplace emergencies derailing static study calendars.
3. **Cognitive Load & Fatigue-Aware Pacing:** Dynamically adjusts study session lengths and break intervals based on subject difficulty and self-reported mental energy.
   - *Persona:* Chloe Davis
   - *Pain Point Solved:* Rigid timers that treat all subjects equally, triggering task paralysis and executive dysfunction during complex assignments.
4. **Planned vs. Actual Study Duration Tracking:** Logs actual completion times against planned estimates to calibrate future scheduling algorithms.
   - *Persona:* Maya Lin and Chloe Davis
   - *Pain Point Solved:* Inability to accurately estimate task durations, leading to schedule collapse and pre-exam cramming.

### Nice-To-Have
1. **Two-Way External Calendar Sync:** Integrates bi-directionally with Google Calendar and Outlook to automatically block out lectures, work shifts, and personal commitments.
   - *Persona:* Marcus Vance
   - *Pain Point Solved:* Manual double-entry between corporate calendars and personal study tools.
2. **Break-Term Habit Maintenance Mode:** Lightweight daily habit tracking and reading routines designed to retain user engagement between academic terms.
   - *Persona:* Maya Lin
   - *Pain Point Solved:* Total loss of organizational momentum and high churn during semester breaks.

## Pricing Tiers

| Tier | Monthly Price | Best For | Includes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Free** | $0 | Casual students exploring structured study habits | Manual task scheduling, standard Pomodoro timer, 1 syllabus upload per term, 7-day schedule view. |
| **Pro** | $8 | Full-time undergraduate and graduate students managing multi-course workloads | Unlimited syllabus ingestion, AI dynamic rebalancing, fatigue-aware pacing, planned vs. actual analytics, calendar sync. |
| **Team** | $18 | Study groups, thesis pods, and campus academic cohorts (per user/month) | All Pro features, plus shared assignment milestones, group study session scheduling, and collaborative progress tracking. |

## Positioning Statement
For university students who struggle to manage unstructured course workloads and unpredictable schedules, Stride is an AI-powered study planning mobile app that dynamically adapts daily study routines to real-time academic progress and cognitive energy. Unlike basic timer apps and enterprise productivity tools, our product automatically translates course syllabi into flexible schedules that absorb disruptions without manual replanning.

## Launch Timeline

| Week | Goal | Key Activities |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Week 1** | Beta Readiness & Core Validation | Deploy closed beta to 50 undergraduate and graduate test users; benchmark syllabus parser accuracy across common document formats. |
