# Market Research Report
## Product Idea
The product idea is a smart water bottle designed to track hydration levels specifically for university students.

## Problem Statement
University students often face challenges in maintaining proper hydration, which can impact their academic performance and overall well-being. This smart water bottle aims to solve the problem of inadequate hydration by providing a convenient and effective way to monitor and remind students to drink enough water throughout the day.

## Target Market
- **Primary Segment:** University students aged 18-24, both male and female, who are tech-savvy and value health and wellness.
- **Secondary Segment:** College athletes and part-time workers who are also university students.
- **Market Size:** The market for health and wellness products among university students is large and growing, driven by increasing awareness of health issues and the adoption of smart technology.

## Market Gaps & Opportunities
1. **Integration with university campus services:** Competitors do not offer integration with university services such as meal plans or health services.
2. **Personalized hydration goals:** Many products do not offer personalized hydration goals based on individual needs and activities.
3. **Social and community features:** There is a lack of social and community features that encourage hydration among peers.

## Risks & Mitigations
| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |

| **Technical Risk:** Potential issues with Bluetooth connectivity and app performance. | **Mitigation:** Conduct thorough testing and offer software updates. |
| **Regulatory Risk:** Compliance with health and safety regulations. | **Mitigation:** Ensure compliance with relevant regulations and obtain necessary certifications. |
