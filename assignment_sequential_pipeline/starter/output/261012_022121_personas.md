# Customer Personas

## Persona 1: Alex (Computer Science Major)
- Age: 21
- Background: Alex is a 21-year-old Computer Science major who juggles a full course load with a part-time job at the campus library.
- Goals: 
  1. Improve academic performance by staying hydrated.
  2. Track daily water intake to maintain a healthy lifestyle.
  3. Connect with friends to challenge each other in staying hydrated.
- Pain Points: 
  1. Forgets to drink water regularly due to busy schedule.
  2. Lacks motivation to drink enough water on a daily basis.
  3. No easy way to track hydration with friends.
- Buying Triggers: 
  1. Affordable price and integration with popular fitness apps.
  2. Sleek and modern design that fits in with tech-savvy lifestyle.
  3. Ability to set personalized hydration goals and reminders.
- Quote: 'I need something that reminds me to drink water without adding to my stress.'


## Persona 2: Jamie (College Athlete)
- Age: 20
- Background: Jamie is a 20-year-old track athlete who needs to maintain optimal hydration for peak performance.
- Goals: 
  1. Ensure proper hydration before, during, and after workouts.
  2. Monitor hydration levels to prevent dehydration during competitions.
  3. Get personalized hydration recommendations based on training intensity.
- Pain Points: 
  1. Difficulty keeping track of water intake during long training sessions.
  2. Struggles with dehydration during competitions, affecting performance.
  3. Lack of specific hydration goals for athletic needs.
- Buying Triggers: 
  1. Advanced hydration tracking algorithms.
  2. Long battery life to last through entire training sessions.
  3. Ability to integrate with health services on campus for additional support.
- Quote: 'I need a reliable way to stay hydrated and perform at my best.'


## Persona 3: Casey (Part-Time Worker)
- Age: 23
- Background: Casey is a 23-year-old part-time worker and full-time student who often skips meals and forgets to drink water.
- Goals: 
  1. Stay hydrated throughout the day to maintain energy levels.
  2. Receive reminders to drink water at regular intervals.
  3. Track hydration to ensure it aligns with a balanced diet.
- Pain Points: 
  1. Forgets to drink water due to a busy schedule.
  2. Struggles to maintain a balanced diet and hydration.
  3. Lacks social support to stay motivated.
- Buying Triggers: 
  1. Affordable price and stylish design.
  2. Integration with meal plans and health services on campus.
  3. Social features to connect with peers and stay motivated.
- Quote: 'I need a way to stay hydrated and healthy without it feeling like a chore.'
